Website Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Information you choose to send

The contact widget runs in your browser. It does not submit information to an Aqua Care website server. If you choose WhatsApp or Email, the widget opens that service with your message prepared. You decide whether to send it. WhatsApp/Meta or your email provider then handles the information under its own privacy terms.

Website and asset requests

When you visit, the website host may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, requested page, and request time, and may keep server logs under its own policies. The homepage requests some images from Pexels, and the site requests fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical request information when your browser loads those assets.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, accounts, or cookies for its own tracking. Your browser or the external services you choose may have their own storage and privacy practices.

Your choices

You can use the site without filling in the contact widget. If you send an enquiry and want to ask about the information in it, contact aquacaretrading67@gmail.com.

Changes and questions

This notice may be updated when the website or its data practices change. For privacy questions, contact Aqua Care Trading.
