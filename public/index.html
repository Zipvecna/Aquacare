About Company

Aqua Care

The Water Treatment Company

Established in 2012, Aqua Care is a leading provider of industrial water treatment chemicals and solutions in Bangladesh. Over the years, we have grown into a trusted technical partner for manufacturing plants, power facilities, and large-scale industrial operations across the country.

We specialize in Cooling Water, Boiler Water, Industrial Water, Waste Water, Drinking Water, and Reverse Osmosis (RO) Water Treatment, supported by high performance chemicals, specialized additives, and customized treatment programs.

Aqua Care • Est. 2012

Our experienced team of chemists works directly with industries, providing on-site water analysis, system monitoring, chemical dosing and technical support. By combining quality products with expert field service, we deliver reliable, tailored solutions that improve operational efficiency, protect critical equipment, and maintain consistent water quality.
